# What Changed brand guide

**Evidence state: available design specification; Windows tray inspection remains a
release-candidate manual check.**

The mark is a “Delta Bridge”: a muted before bar and an accent after bar are
offset by one radius and joined by a narrow bridge. It means a change crossing
from a known-good state to the present; it is not a shield, cloud sync icon,
antivirus badge or automatic repair promise.

## Tokens

- App mark ink: `#0B0E14`
- Before bar: `#3A4459`
- After bar: `#4FA3FF`
- Bridge: `#E6E9EF`
- Minimum clear space: one bridge stroke on every side.
- Preferred minimum: 16 px for the tray, 20 px for compact controls, 24 px
  for taskbar surfaces, 32 px for product chrome.

The vector master is [`assets/brand/icon-source.svg`](../assets/brand/icon-source.svg);
`delta-bridge.svg` is a compatibility alias. Tray variants may use their
surface-specific light/dark/high-contrast colours, but retain the same offset
bar-and-bridge silhouette. Raster variants must be regenerated by the checked-in
exporter; do not hand-edit an ICO or PNG. The current high-contrast source is a
black/white/yellow static fallback; system-palette adaptation and installed
Windows inspection remain open. Clear space applies around the mark in a
lockup, not inside the app tile.

The [one-colour source](../assets/brand/logo-mark-monochrome.svg) is white for
dark surfaces only. Installer header/sidebar and the draft article cover have
versioned SVG sources under `assets/brand/`; the Windows BMPs and public rasters
are exporter outputs. The [review sheet](../assets/designs/delta-bridge-approval-sheet.svg)
labels each source but is not human approval for publication.

Voice is calm, plain and evidence-led: “what changed”, “we found evidence”,
and “we cannot tell” are preferred. Avoid “root cause”, “fixed”, “AI”, and
absolute security claims.
