# Launch messaging

**Evidence state: available fact sheet; release-specific hashes and URLs are planned in the P25 packet.**

This is the single fact sheet for public copy. Technical truth remains in the
root documentation and release audit.

## Hierarchy

1. **Problem:** “It worked before. What changed?”
2. **Promise:** a plain-language local history of meaningful Windows change.
3. **Proof:** bounded collectors, deterministic evidence ranking, checkpoints,
   and a visible privacy boundary.
4. **Limits:** no causal certainty, no elevation, no runtime network, and
   known blind spots are named.
5. **Call to action:** try the unsigned Windows preview and read the checksum.

## Anti-claims

Never say “root cause”, “caused”, “fixes your PC”, “AI”, “real-time
everything”, “zero memory”, “fully secure”, or “replacement for Process
Monitor”. What Changed keeps persistent cross-source history and evidence
ranking; Process Monitor traces raw operations. They serve different jobs.
