# Community post variants

**Evidence state: available copy; publication is deferred until a single launch approval names the RC.**

## Short English

It worked before. What changed? What Changed keeps a local, plain-language
history of meaningful Windows changes and ranks related evidence around a
symptom. Sensitive values are redacted; causal certainty is not claimed. The
unsigned preview, limitations and checksums are documented in the repository.
