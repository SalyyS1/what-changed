# What Changed brand guide

**Evidence state: available; installed Windows inspection remains a known limitation until the RC walk.**

## Meaning

Delta Bridge shows a known-before state, the change marker, and the current-after
state. It is deliberately not a radar, shield, cloud-sync badge, AI sparkle or
automatic repair promise.

## Tokens and use

- Ink: `#0B0E14`; slate: `#3A4459`; bridge: `#E6E9EF`; accent: `#4FA3FF`.
- Keep at least one bridge-stroke of clear space around the mark in lockups.
- Use the light/dark tray variants on their matching surfaces. The current
  high-contrast source is a static black/white/yellow fallback; adaptive
  Windows palette behavior remains an installed-release review item.
- Minimum sizes: 16 px tray, 20 px compact UI, 24 px taskbar, 32 px app tile.
  Never add a gradient,
  drop shadow, badge, lock, shield or decorative telemetry marks.

## Voice

Calm, concrete and evidence-led. Say “recorded change” and “related evidence”;
do not say “root cause”, “fixes your PC”, “AI”, “real-time everything” or
“fully secure”.
